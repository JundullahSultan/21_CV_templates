# Click-to-edit: real keyboard and touch checks

**Physical Pashto keyboards, Android Chrome and iOS Safari caret/selection behavior remain unverified until you report results.** Automated tests use real text and synthetic composition events, not an installed OS keyboard.

1. Open the built app on a desktop and on an actual phone/tablet. Record device, OS, browser, viewport, template ID and keyboard/input-method name. Test templates 01, 13 and 21, then your preferred template.
2. On desktop with a fine pointer and wide window, confirm there is no Edit CV button. Click your name, job title and organization independently. Type mixed case, blur to save, Enter to save, Shift+Enter for a newline and Escape to cancel. CSS uppercase must not change the saved spelling. Test Ctrl/Cmd+Z, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z, and Ctrl+Y.
3. On a phone or coarse-pointer tablet, confirm Edit CV is initially off. Tap text and scroll from directly over a field: neither should open an editor. Turn Edit CV on. Scroll again, then tap a small field: only the stationary tap should open a bottom sheet. Confirm the input remains readable without iOS input zoom. Type, check the live draft, Cancel, then repeat and press Done. Test the toolbar Undo/Redo buttons after Done. Turn editing off and verify taps only view/scroll.
4. Install/enable the actual Pashto keyboard in OS keyboard settings. Type this directly rather than pasting: `زه د کارن تجربې ډیزاینر یم`. Add English `UX Design / React 2026` in the middle. Place the caret between Pashto words, insert a word, select across the Pashto/English boundary, replace the selection and use Backspace. Confirm joining, caret placement and selection remain correct. Repeat in Dari: `من با پژوهش راه‌حل‌های روشن می‌سازم`. Preserve the ZWNJ between `راه` and `حل` and in `می‌سازم`.
5. If the keyboard offers composition/candidate selection, press Enter to accept a candidate: this must not save the field prematurely. Finish composition, then save. Test Escape on desktop while composition is finished.
6. Paste a richly formatted paragraph from a website. Confirm only plain text appears. Test multiline paste, a bullet Enter, and Backspace on an empty bullet. Confirm one Undo restores the whole saved change.
7. Click a date range. Choose From year, month and day, then To year, month and day. Test February 2024 (29 days), year-only dates and Present. Cancel a change and reopen it to confirm the saved date was retained.
8. Add long achievements and optional sections until continuation pages appear. Confirm complete entries move, A4 proportions remain stable and no content is clipped at either page edge. Chat must open as a drawer and close without moving the CV into a side column. Test both portrait and landscape orientation.
9. Finish the active edit, then Download PDF. Compare it with Preview and check that edits, dates, bullets and extra sections are retained; no outlines, editing buttons or field labels should appear. Follow the [PDF device checklist](pdf-mobile-tests.md) for search/copy and font tests.

Report device/OS/browser, keyboard name, template, language, and which numbered checks passed or failed. Include the exact string, caret position and reproduction steps for any RTL input issue.
